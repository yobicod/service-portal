# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy and never share credentials or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information, or any conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
